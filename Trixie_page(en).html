Trixie Lulamoon

Trixie Lulamoon is a major supporting character in Tamers12345's My Little Pony Friendship is Magic continuation. She is a traveling magician, the pupil of Princess Luna, and rival turned lover of Twilight Sparkle.

Appearance

Trixie is a blue unicorn with purple eyes and a white mane and tail. She wears a purple cape with a light blue jewel serving as the clasp, as well as a pointed purple hat to hide the fact that she is already balding in her 20s. Her hat is also the most expensive thing she owns at $2, and it used to have a bunch of star stickers on it before they were washed off in the rain after she went outside to take a piss.

Story

Background


Trixie was born to a pair of siblings, and it is believed that she had a twin that died in the womb, allowing her to inherent its power. As a filly, Trixie never fit in with other foals, so she created the great and powerful Trixie persona as a means to cope. She developed a rivalry with Twilight at a young age after she showed her up at show and tell by bringing $6 Hot-N-Ready Pizza from Little Caesar's and mocking the homemade lasagna she made with her mom.

Early cameos


Trixie made two minor appearances before she debuted proper. She was shown falling into a pit during the end of Equestria storyline, and was in the background at Rainbow Dash's party at the roller skating rink.

Twilight Sparkle Shows Trixie The True Meaning Of Friendship Whether She Likes It Or Not


Trixie acts like a nuisance towards Twilight by attempting to blow her up with a grenade and cutting the power to her house. After the community at large stops Trixie from performing on the street, Pinkie Pie allows her to do her magic act by the restroom of Sugarcube Corner. The rest of the Mane Six, minus Twilight, show up at the bakery and are seemingly very impressed with Trixie's illusion magic. Pinkie proceeds to invite her on a picnic with her friends, but it turns out it was all a ploy to get back at her for picking on Twilight. They kneecap her multiple times, steal her hat, and worst of all, fill it with cheese and force her to wear it, causing her to run off in tears.
Twilight visits Trixie's wagon and attempts to befriend her by taking her on a picnic, but Trixie refuses to go with her. Pinkie and her friends arrive not long after to harass her some more. A combination of kicks and farts from Applejack causes her wagon to fall off the edge of the cliff. Twilight manages to pull the wagon back up, but Trixie attempts to kill herself anyway, citing how her life is awful due to the constant humiliation she endures, her lack of money, and being a product of incest. She jumps, but Twilight saves her by suspending her in the air with a noose before tackling her to the ground.
Trixie thanks Twilight for saving her and goes into her backstory, including the reason why she hates her. Twilight refuses to apologize, but nevertheless orders her friends stop bullying Trixie. The two have a picnic together and bond as the sun sets. In a post-credit scene, Luna is seen taking a great interest in Trixie, and sees much potential within her.

My Little Pony The Movie: The Death Of Twilight Sparkle


Luna contacts Trixie in her dream, explaining how she sees great potential in her and offers to make her her apprentice. After initially declining, Trixie decides to accept Luna's offer. After she awakens, Princess Celestia explains that Luna used a spell on her called the Night's Veil, and now she and Luna are bound together by fate until death.
Trixie goes to Twilight's house, seeking help with removing the spell. Twilight explains that the spell is permanent, but Rarity puts forth the idea of simply moving the spell to another pony besides Luna. Trixie is reluctant when Twilight offers to make herself the new target for the spell, as it would mean she'd be able to see all of her thoughts, but ultimately goes through with it. Rarity uses a transfer spell on the two, but they both seemingly die via spontaneous combustion not long after.
It is later revealed that Trixie and Twilight did not die, and were merely separated from their bodies and transported to the mind of Flawless Sparklemoon. After Flawless appears in her mind plain as well, she attempts to kill Trixie and a magical battle ensues. After Celestia gains the upper hand in her battle against Nightmare Moon, Luna appears in Flawless's mind as well, and the three horses momentarily defeat the mare in the mirror by impaling her three-way with their horns as Celestia rescues them from her mind. That night, Trixie informs Twilight that she's leaving Ponyville with Luna for a while so they can work out the stability of the spell she placed on her. Twilight offers to let Trixie stay at her house when she eventually returns, and the two proceed to make love under the stars.

My Little Pony The Movie: The Death Of Trixie Lulamoon


Trixie finds out she has a long lost twin sister by the name of Marionette who was born sickly as Trixie accidentally stole her magic while in the womb. Marionette convnces Trixie to kill their father Jackpot because of him abandoning their mother. Marionette then betrays her revealing her plan to steal Trixie's magic. Trixie ascends to become a Pegacorn and temporarily kills her sister Marionette causing Marionette severe burns.

Flawless FARTS Open a PORTAL To ANOTHER DIMENSION?!! 0c0


After finding out her daughter Flawless farted open a portal to another dimension she goes through the portal to investigate leading to the Sonic Underground universe, specifically the midwest this lead to a series of adventures including prophecy, battles, and her sister Marionette Lulamoon being revived. After she, Twilight, and Flawless get kicked out of the Sonic Underground universe she and Twilight get married after which Trixie tries and fails to run away and divorce Twilight as part of a revenge plot to get half of her things and continuously re-marry and divorce twilight over and over. The plan fails when Trixie's rebuilt wagon falls off a cliff and she is saved by Twilight. Trixie then confesses that she was just scared and insecure and agrees to stay with Twilight and raise their daughter together. Now Trixie, Twilight, Flawless, and Marionette live together in Twilight's treehouse.

Abilities

Illusions


Trixie is a great illusionist. She can do both small magic tricks for example: faking her own death and then appearing behind an opponent's back.

Magic power


Trixie has a powerful magical potential. Apart from normal spells such as deflecting rocks flying at her or conjuring a cannon, as a little filly she was able to kill 36 of her classmates with magic.

Regeneration


Trixie has the ability to fast regenerate thanks to her magic, as seen when she healed the open fracture caused by Applejack's gunshot several times in a row.

Pegacorn God Mode


Trixie has the ability to transform into a Pegacorn God at will

Relationships

Twilight Sparkle: Since the lasagna incident in school, Trixie has declared herself Twilight's rival and has made her life miserable, such as by putting a grenade in her mailbox or cutting electricity. Despite this, thanks to Pinkie Pie's advice, Twilight decided to befriend her. She saved her from suicide and protected from the insults of her friends. From the moment they went on a picnic together and ate sandwiches, it can see their mutual romantic feelings. During their meeting at Unicorn Con in the bathroom, both blush and are embarrassed. Twilight even confesses her previously unknown feelings to Rarity. However, during Fluttershy's hot pot party, she denies that they are friends and is mean to her. Finally, thanks to defeating their fusion, they dared to confess their feelings and became lovers.
Since then Trixie and Twilight have become married.
Princess Luna: Trixie has admired Luna since childhood, as seen when she dressed herself as Nightmare Moon on a Nightmare Night. Luna saw the potential for magic in Trixie and after defeating Flawless, she became her mentor and even friend. They spent time together at a cafe and Luna even confessed to her the truth about her forbidden feelings for Celestia.
Flawless Sparklemoon: Initially an enemy Trixie has come to see her as her daughter.
Marionette Lulamoon: Formerly long lost twin sister. They were initially enemies but have become very close in the movie Flawless FARTS Open a PORTAL To ANOTHER DIMENSION?!! 0c0
