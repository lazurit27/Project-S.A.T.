About

Flawless Sparklemoon

“Twilight was a brilliant mind held back by rules, and Trixie was nothing more than a showmare wishing to be the best. I am the perfect union of their virtues: Twilight's intelligence, Trixie's charisma, but none of their flaws. I am, you could say, Flawless.“

― Flawless's introductory speech



Flawless Sparklemoon was the main antagonist of My Little Pony The Movie: The Death Of Twilight Sparkle. She was a fusion of Twilight Sparkle and Trixie Lulamoon, born after Rarity failed to move the Night's Veil from Princess Luna to Twilight. Only referred to as The Mare in the Mirror in the episode, the community decided on a name for her afterwards. Flawless was chosen as her given name, based on her introductory speech, and her surname is a portmanteau of Twilight and Trixie's last names.

Appearance

Like her components, Flawless is a unicorn. She has a pastel blue coat and purple eyes, and the reflections in her eyes are star shaped. Her mane contains colors ranging from light to dark purple, as well as some pink, while her tail only contains a light and middling shade of purple. Pinkie Pie described her hair as looking like confetti. She wears a black top hat with a purple band, and a black cape.

Story

Main article: My Little Pony The Movie: The Death Of Twilight Sparkle


Following the supposed deaths of Twilight and Trixie, Flawless emerged from a nearby mirror. Pinkie Pie was the first to approach her, insulting her haircut, and was immediately killed. She then impaled Applejack through her neck, and tossed her body in front of Rarity's line of fire when she tried shooting her. Princesses Celestia and Luna arrive not long after, and explain the nature of the fusion to Rainbow Dash and Rarity.
Flawless then teleports away without a trace before the Princesses have the opportunity to do battle with her. Taking an interest in the new horse's magical power, Luna considers the possibility of taking her under her wing as a student. Her suggestion pisses off Celestia, who begins choking her, and Luna fires a bolt of magic at her sister. She then transforms into Nightmare Moon and the two engage in battle. In the distance, Flawless watches on, pleased, seeing their conflict as more proof of her perfection.
She is then summoned into her own mind dimension after Twilight, who is trapped there with Trixie, gets the idea to just ask to leave. After explaining who she is, Flawless appears to kill Trixie, but it turns out to be an illusion, and Trixie taunts her. Flawless and Trixie have a magical duel, and Twilight tries to help but is easily dispatched after Flawless simply kicks her away. After Trixie survives two more of her magical attacks, Twilight and Trixie explain to Flawless how she is not perfect at all, and is merely a cheap imitation of them both.
After Celestia gains the upper hand against Nightmare Moon, Luna manages to find her way to Flawless's mind as well. Her arrival further upsets Flawless, whose mind begins cracking. In a desperate attempt to take them down with her, Flawless unravels the entirety of the space they reside in, eliminating Luna, Trixie, and Twilight's magic. In response, the three mares gore Flawless three-way with their horns, seemingly killing her. Afterwards, they are rescued by Princess Celestia.
All seems well, but Flawless managed to hold on to life, and opens a portal into Equestria, with the plan to destroy herself and all of Ponyville with a magical explosion. Twilight puts forth the idea of attacking her all at once with their combined magic to stop her. Twilight gathers a total of nine magic users, but just before they can attack, Rarity releases a fart so strong that it knocks everyone off their feet, leaving only her and Sunset Shimmer, who was standing on a hill, to stop the Mare in the Mirror. Luckily, the magic blast from the two is enough to decapitate her.
Flawless was last seen near Twilight and Trixie while they made love, her head back, but still dying nonetheless.

In the episode «My Little Pony: StarSong and Toola Roola Come to Visit», while Rainbow Dash, Twilight, Pinkie and the titular mares enter Carousel Boutique, Flawless' reflection can be seen in the window, replacing Twilight's one.

Super Speed


When she met Twilight and Trixie, she charged at Trixie with such speed that she managed to tear through her body with her hoof, even though it was merely an illusion of Trixie's.

Athleticism


After Trixie parried her magic attack and sent the floating rocks right back at her, Flawless was able to outmaneuver them with a number of jumps and flips, and also managed to kick away Twilight as she dodged.

Reception

Following the release of the episode, Flawless experienced an explosion of popularity, with many fans creating art, songs, and plushies centered around the original character.
